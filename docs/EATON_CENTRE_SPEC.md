Plan origin is the intersection of the Queen and Yonge wall datums. Increasing u goes north and increasing v goes west. The builder uses one stud per horizontal plan foot. The elevations above describe the game. They are not surveyed building heights. A published description places the south street entrance at L2, one level below the north street entrance. The game currently generates a flat sidewalk at both ends. Resolving Queen requires a local grade profile tied to the sidewalk and actual sill, along with a verified section for the lower mall to Queen south concourse.

## Reference evidence

- CF's leasing sheets distinguish the concourse, numbered retail levels and partial floors. H&M is identified by unit B212 across floors, Apple by 2105C on L2, and Foot Locker and Adidas by 1108C and A015 on L1. Tenant identity is reference information. The game catalog supplies fictional public names. The existing registry is a dated snapshot. [CF leasing sheets](https://assets.cadillacfairview.com/m/43273f2554bf438e/original/TECON20_LEASINGPLAN.pdf)
- TTC locates the southwest entrance inside the mall atrium with access to the southbound platform. The southeast square entrance serves northbound. Stairs and elevators reach an unpaid underpass. The current game's generic fare hall and island platform differ from that arrangement. [TMU Station](https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/TMU-station)
- TTC locates Queen's mall entrance inside the building, 32 metres west of Yonge on Queen, with access through the lower mall to the south concourse. This is distinct from the adjacent sidewalk entrance. [Queen Station](https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/queen-station)
- The published north/south grade relationship is secondary evidence. A dimensioned section would establish finished-floor heights and how each intermediate street entrance meets the grade. [Toronto Eaton Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto_Eaton_Centre)

## Routes and limits of the reconstruction

The north route starts at the glazed drum on Yonge, beside the rounded fashion storefront. The north L3–L2 escalator top is near (1333,42) and its lower landing near (1301,62). The next bank down reaches L1 near (1318,71). Continue south on L1 for the concourse descent. The station passage branches on L2 toward the north boundary. The public door glazing, storefront glazing, model escalator endpoints, and floor plates can be inspected individually in source. Clear walking widths, visual sightlines, collision, and a complete avatar journey still require a generated-place walkthrough.

For validation, follow the public doors past the fashion anchor, down to Urban Eatery, back up through the galleria to the partial L4, and south to Queen. Separately test each station from the mall, including the accessible routes. Verify the two food halls, atrium bridges, exterior approaches, actual lift stops and underpass against current interior imagery. The current mall link configuration has one station target (Dundas); Queen does not yet have a dedicated physical mall link. The unused exterior well helper is inactive because the link supplies no well rectangle.
