### Interior finishes

The photo set is the colour reference. The public floors use pale warm stone with darker court inlays. The north court pairs a cool grey panel field and red square signs at the UNIQLO-inspired frontage with a near-black band and green lettering at the Simons-inspired passage. The fountain-court bookstore uses honey-brown panels and pale serif lettering. The Zara-inspired frontage uses pale cream panels, dark narrow joints and a smaller charcoal sign. All signs display the fictional tenant names assigned to their units. These are visual matches by eye. Lighting and camera exposure vary across the photos, and the panel spacing and colours have not been measured on site.

### Shooting fountain

The circle opposite A015 on the 3500 × 2265 L1 raster centres near pixel (1514.5,1490.5), radius 26.5 pixels. Queen is x=578 and Yonge is y=1816 at two pixels per foot. The resulting centre is **(468.25,162.75)** and the outer circle is 26.5 feet wide. The drawing leaves that circle's material boundary unlabeled. ### Circulation validation

An earlier Studio playtest used a normal Humanoid from the Yonge sidewalk through the public drum. The avatar reached L2. Approaching the actual down lanes brought it to root Y=-33.77 on L1 and then Y=-51.54 on the Urban Eatery concourse. Intermediate navigation targets stalled on an opposing lane and on the L1 approach. The walk succeeded after retargeting. This establishes a physically traversable descent in that build. First-time wayfinding, real escalator directions and sightlines on arrival remain open. The indexed walking tour identifies the entrance at 1:55 and Urban Eatery at 20:36. Its frames were unavailable for verification. [Walking tour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWh_XQOmvBI)

TMU keeps the DUNDAS train key, has outside boarding edges on both sides and directional fare lines at platform level. Its generated entrance mezzanines are at -17, platforms at -31 and unpaid crossing at -49. Street approaches and the mall passage returned successful platform path queries in Studio. A full elevator trip, fare interaction and live boarding in each direction remain untested. The landing lengths, lift positions and underpass depth are gameplay dimensions. TTC lists five entrances. [TTC TMU](https://www.ttc.ca/subway-stations/TMU-station)
